# Privacy Policy
**Effective Date**: February 07, 2026

## 1. Information We Collect
The Alliance Event Bot collects minimal data necessary to provide its core functionality. This includes:

- **Server Information**: Guild IDs and names to distinguish settings between different Discord servers.

- **Channel Information**: Channel IDs where events are posted or role menus are displayed.

- **Role Information**: Role IDs and names created or managed by the bot for alliance pings.

- **User Information**: Discord User IDs (only when assigned a specific permission level within the bot or when using the gift-code redeemer).

- **Event Data**: Information provided by users regarding scheduled events (names, dates, times).

## 2. How We Use Information
We use the collected data solely for the following purposes:

- To coordinate and ping alliance events.

- To manage automated role assignments via dropdown menus.

- To facilitate the gift-code redemption process as requested by the user.

- To maintain server-specific settings (e.g., restricted channels).

## 3. Data Storage and Retention
- **Storage**: All data is stored in a local SQLite database controlled by the developer.

- **Backups**: As stated in the Terms of Service, there are no guaranteed backups. Data may be lost during technical failures or updates.

- **Retention**: Data is stored as long as the bot is part of your server. If the bot is kicked or banned, the data remains in the database until a manual cleanup is performed by the developer.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties
- **No Selling**: We never sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties.

- **Discord API**: Data is transmitted through the Discord API to perform bot actions (sending messages, managing roles).

## 5. Your Rights
Users and Server Administrators can:

- **Request Deletion**: You can request the removal of your server's data by contacting the developer and removing the bot from your server.

- **Access**: You can view stored event data and channel mappings using the provided bot commands (e.g., /event_list, /list_alliance_channels).

## 6. Contact
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer directly via Discord (Undertakehd).
